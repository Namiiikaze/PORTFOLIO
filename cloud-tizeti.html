Tizeti Cloud

Sovereign cloud infrastructure built for Nigeria, eliminating overseas data routing, dollar exposure, and compliance risk for Nigerian businesses in a single platform.

Role
Product Design
Platform
Web app
Focus
Infrastructure & data sovereignty

Overview

Tizeti Cloud is a sovereign cloud infrastructure platform built to solve the specific technical and economic challenges facing Nigerian businesses. Nigerian companies were routing local traffic through European data centers, adding up to 150ms of avoidable latency, exposing sensitive data to foreign jurisdictions, and paying infrastructure bills in dollars at the mercy of volatile exchange rates. Tizeti Cloud fixes all three: sub-10ms local latency, full NDPR/CBN/NITDA compliance, and Naira-only billing with no FX exposure.

Project objectives

  1. 01Give Nigerian businesses a clear, confident path to move their infrastructure local, without technical complexity.
  2. 02Communicate data sovereignty and compliance (NDPR, CBN, NITDA) as a feature, not a footnote.
  3. 03Make Naira-only pricing feel like a relief, not a compromise, turning currency stability into a selling point.
  4. 04Surface uptime, latency and infrastructure health in a way that builds trust at a glance.
  5. 05Design tiered plans (SME, Fintech, Enterprise) that map clearly to the needs and scale of each customer segment.
