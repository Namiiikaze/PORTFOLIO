About

From a curiosity in 2023 to shipping products with real teams.

I'm Ayobami Shoyombo, a product designer based in Lagos, Nigeria. My degree is in International Law and Diplomacy. Design was something I taught myself, then kept chasing until it became the work. Today I design across web and mobile, obsess over the small details that make products feel human, and I'm at my best taking an idea from MVP to launch alongside founding and engineering teams.

The journey

  1. 2023

    Where it started

    Product design began as a curiosity in 2023. With no formal design background, I taught myself the fundamentals (user flows, wireframing, and the principles behind interfaces that actually work), building my eye one project at a time.

  2. 2025

    TechCrush bootcamp

    A scholarship took me to the TechCrush UI/UX Design Bootcamp, where self-study finally met structure. I designed Farm Input, an agricultural product concept, and graduated with a foundation I could build a career on.

  3. Apr to Jun 2026

    The HNG Internship

    I joined the HNG Internship and worked through every stage, 0 to 7. In a team through stages 3 and 4, I designed Seil, a guided marketing funnel. I built Lorekeeper, a worldbuilding workspace, in stage 5, and NConnect, an event-networking app, in stage 6, then reached the final stage and shipped a vibe-coded web app of my own.

  4. 2024 → Now

    Designing real products

    At Tizeti, where I've been a product designer since November 2024, I was handedTizeti Cloud, a brand-new sovereign-cloud product, to shape from the ground up. Alongside it, client work: enrolment, testimonial and admin experiences forRexcel, a live online academy; an e-commerce build for the interior brand Ubliss; and, as a volunteer, a community platform for CX Naija.

  5. Now

    Motion, graphics & what's next

    Lately I've been dabbling in motion design, and I'm building Offmark, a tool for fashion designers, while sharpening my graphics and motion skills. The brief I set myself hasn't changed: design, build, ship.
